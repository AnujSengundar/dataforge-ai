# Product Vision

DataForge AI Studio is a SaaS-style self-service analytics platform for turning raw business files into trusted, analysis-ready data products.

The product allows users to upload CSV or Excel files, inspect data quality, infer structure, generate data profiles, load data into PostgreSQL, create warehouse-ready models, surface business insights, and eventually interact with the dataset through an AI assistant.

The core vision is to compress the journey from raw file to business intelligence. Instead of forcing users to manually inspect spreadsheets, clean inconsistent fields, design database tables, write exploratory queries, and build reports from scratch, DataForge AI Studio provides a guided data workspace that automates the repetitive parts while preserving transparency and control.

## Why It Is Useful

### Freelancers

Freelance data analysts often receive messy client files with unclear structure, missing documentation, inconsistent column names, and limited business context. DataForge AI Studio helps them profile data faster, identify quality issues, produce repeatable outputs, and deliver cleaner dashboards or reports.

### Analysts

Analysts spend significant time preparing data before insight generation begins. The platform helps standardize profiling, metadata capture, staging, schema inference, and basic insight generation so analysts can focus on interpretation and decision support.

### Founders

Startup founders and operators often have important data in exports from sales, marketing, finance, support, or product tools. DataForge AI Studio helps them move from scattered CSV files to structured insights without needing a full data team.

### Small Businesses

Small businesses need practical reporting but may not have data engineering resources. The product provides a path from spreadsheet-based operations to structured analytics, with PostgreSQL-backed storage and Power BI-ready outputs.

## Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal is to build an AI-powered self-service analytics studio.

In its mature form, DataForge AI Studio should help users upload operational data, understand data quality, build warehouse-ready models, generate dashboards, ask natural-language questions, run ML-assisted analysis, and export executive-ready reports from one integrated workspace.
