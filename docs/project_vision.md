# Project Vision: DataForge AI

DataForge AI is an end-to-end data project that turns raw real-world data into useful intelligence. It is designed to show how data can move through a complete modern analytics system: ingestion, storage, transformation, warehousing, dashboards, machine learning, APIs, and AI-assisted insights.

In simple terms, DataForge AI will collect and organize market data, analyze patterns, and present insights that help people make better decisions.

## First Domain: Job Market Intelligence

The first domain selected for DataForge AI is Job Market Intelligence.

This means the project will focus on job postings, skills, salaries, locations, companies, experience levels, and hiring trends. The goal is to understand what the job market is asking for and convert that information into practical insights.

## Target Users

DataForge AI can be useful for several types of users:

- Job seekers who want to understand which skills are in demand.
- Recruiters who want to track hiring trends and talent-market signals.
- Freelancers who want to identify profitable skills and service opportunities.
- Students who want to plan learning paths based on real job demand.
- Career coaches who want data-backed guidance for clients.

## Why This Project Is Useful

The job market changes quickly. New tools, technologies, and role expectations appear often, and it can be difficult to know which skills are worth learning or hiring for.

DataForge AI helps by turning scattered job-market data into clear answers, such as:

- Which skills are most requested for a role?
- Which tools appear together in job descriptions?
- Which locations or remote roles are growing?
- What experience levels are most common?
- What trends can be seen across time?

This makes the project valuable both as a practical product idea and as a strong portfolio project. It demonstrates data engineering, analytics, machine learning, AI, API development, dashboarding, and real-world business thinking in one connected system.
