# Domain Selection

DataForge AI needs a domain that is realistic, useful, technically rich, and strong enough to support a portfolio-grade project. Four possible domains were considered:

- E-commerce analytics
- GitHub developer intelligence
- Finance intelligence
- Job market intelligence

## Domain Comparison

| Domain | Uniqueness | Data Availability | Resume Value | Freelance Value | AI/ML Potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-commerce analytics | Medium. Common portfolio domain, but still practical. | High. Sample sales datasets are widely available. | Medium. Shows dashboards, KPIs, and warehousing. | Medium. Useful for small business reporting. | Medium. Can support demand forecasting, segmentation, and recommendations. |
| GitHub developer intelligence | High. Interesting for developer-focused analytics. | Medium. GitHub APIs provide useful data, but rate limits and API design must be handled carefully. | High. Strong for software and data engineering roles. | Medium. Useful for developer tooling and open-source insights. | High. Can support repository classification, contributor analysis, and trend detection. |
| Finance intelligence | Medium. Valuable, but many projects use stock-market data. | Medium. Some free APIs and datasets exist, but many high-quality sources are paid or restricted. | High. Finance analytics has strong business appeal. | Medium. Useful, but may require careful handling of financial advice boundaries. | High. Can support forecasting, anomaly detection, and risk analytics. |
| Job market intelligence | High. Practical, relatable, and directly tied to career outcomes. | High. Can start with sample CSV data and later expand to APIs, public datasets, and compliant collection methods. | High. Demonstrates analytics with strong business and career relevance. | High. Useful for resumes, recruiting, career coaching, and freelance market research. | High. Supports skill extraction, job clustering, salary analysis, recommendations, and AI summaries. |

## Why Job Market Intelligence Was Selected

Job Market Intelligence was selected because it is both technically strong and easy to understand. Almost everyone can relate to jobs, skills, hiring, salaries, and career decisions, which makes the project more memorable to reviewers, clients, and users.

This domain also fits the full DataForge AI vision. It can support:

- Data engineering pipelines for job posting ingestion.
- OLTP storage for raw and normalized job data.
- Data warehouse modeling for analytics.
- Power BI dashboards for trends and KPIs.
- Machine learning for skill extraction, classification, clustering, and recommendations.
- FastAPI endpoints for serving insights.
- Ollama-powered AI summaries and question answering.
- MCP integration for exposing project capabilities to AI tools.

Compared with the other domains, Job Market Intelligence provides the best balance of uniqueness, data availability, resume value, freelance value, and AI/ML potential.

## Final Decision

The selected first domain for DataForge AI is Job Market Intelligence.

The project will begin with controlled synthetic or sample CSV data, then later expand into real-world public datasets, APIs, and compliant data collection methods.
